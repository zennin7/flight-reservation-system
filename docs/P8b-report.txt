(1) SeatMapAndCheckoutLayoutTest.testCheckoutLayout (checkout.fxml at 900 & 1100)
  - Root cause:
    The checkout main glass container still carried rigid prefWidth="1168" /
    minWidth="1168", the Confirm button graphic HBox had prefWidth="480", and
    the left column had no responsive shrink range. At widths 900 and 1100, the
    right-hand summary labels ("Demo Traveller", "Demo payment, no real card is
    charged", "Card", "Seat 7C · Economy", price rows, VAT line, "SEAT HOLD")
    overflowed the root bounds by ~25-36 px.
  - Fix:
    * desktop/src/main/resources/com/group2/flightres/fxml/checkout.fxml:
      - Reduced root minWidth to 860 / minHeight to 620 and outer margins to
        4 20 16 20.
      - Removed rigid prefWidth/minWidth="1168" on search-main-glass.
      - Made Left Column responsive (prefWidth="400", minWidth="320",
        maxWidth="500", HBox.hgrow="SOMETIMES") and marked tripHeroImageView
        managed="false" so it never inflates column width.
      - Tightened card preview dimensions (200x136) and removed fixed
        prefWidth="480" from the Confirm button graphic HBox.
    * desktop/src/main/java/com/group2/flightres/controller/CheckoutController.java:
      - Bound tripHeroImageView fitWidth/fitHeight and a rounded Rectangle clip
        dynamically to the hero banner StackPane so the hero photo never bleeds
        across the card boundary at narrow widths.
  - Test untouched. Verified GREEN at 900 and 1100 px.

(2) SeatMapAndCheckoutLayoutTest.testSeatMapLayout (seat-map.fxml at 900 & 1100)
  - Root cause:

    available seat on the map.") had prefWidth="136" while its available width
    inside cabinPhotoCard was 135.2 px, causing a 0.8 px truncation violation.
    Additionally, at 900 px the aircraft graphic slightly overlapped the right
    column and vertical spacing pushed row F and the Flight Card near the bottom.
  - Fix:
    * desktop/src/main/resources/com/group2/flightres/fxml/seat-map.fxml:
      - Set wrapText="true" prefWidth="128" maxWidth="132" minHeight="-Infinity"
        on the cabin description label so it wraps cleanly inside cabinPhotoCard.
      - Reduced root minWidth to 860 / minHeight to 620, margins to 4 20 16 20.
      - Compacted seat-plane-card (height 170, planeGraphic scale 0.58) and
        aligned cabinPhotoCard prefHeight/maxHeight/clip to 360 px so the full
        cabin description, seat rows A-F, and Flight Card fit cleanly with zero
        overlap or cutoff.
  - Test untouched. Verified GREEN at 900 and 1100 px.

  - Test untouched. Verified GREEN at 1280, 1100, and 900 px.
