# Make it feel human

## Words
- Short, specific, warm. Say what happens: "Your seat is held for 10 minutes" not "Operation successful".
- Errors say what went wrong and what to do: "That seat was just taken. Pick another one."
- Use the person's data: "Hi Thabo, you are flying to Cape Town on Friday".
- Banned phrases: "seamless", "cutting edge", "unlock the power", "elevate your journey", "revolutionary", "world class", "in today's fast paced world".
- No emojis as icons. No exclamation marks in errors. Sentence case for buttons.

## Images
- One consistent photo style: warm golden hour, natural light, real places (Cape Town, Johannesburg, Durban, Victoria Falls). No airline logos or liveries in photos.
- Crop to the same aspect ratio per slot (hero 16:9, destination card 4:3, login side panel 3:4).
- Overlay a navy gradient (about 55 percent at the text side) for readable text.
- Keep a credits list in docs/photo-credits.md (photographer, site, link, licence).

## Layout polish
- Align to one spacing scale. Max three font sizes per screen. One primary action per screen.
- Empty states have a friendly sentence and a next action. Loading states show a skeleton, not a blank page.
- Details people notice: consistent corner radius, 1px borders with low contrast, shadows that are soft and the same everywhere.

## Check before finishing
- Read every visible sentence aloud. If it could appear on any company's site, rewrite it for this airline and this route.
- Compare light and dark side by side. Fix anything that only works in one.
